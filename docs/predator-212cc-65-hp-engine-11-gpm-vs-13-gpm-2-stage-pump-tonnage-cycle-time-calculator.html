Frequently Asked Questions (Predator 212cc (6.5 HP) Engine + 11 GPM vs 13 GPM 2-Stage Pump)

How many true tons of force does a 4.0-inch cylinder produce at 3,500 PSI?

Using Pascal's Law, multiply piston area ($\pi \times [4.0 \div 2]^2 = 12.57\text{ sq. in.}$) by 3,500 PSI to get 43,995 lbs of force. Dividing by 2,000 lbs/ton equals 22.0 True Hydraulic Tons.

What is the cycle time of a 4.0" × 24" cylinder with a 11 GPM pump?

The extend stroke holds 1.31 gallons and the retract stroke holds 1.06 gallons (because the 1.75" rod takes up space), totaling 2.37 gallons per full cycle. At 11 GPM, it takes 7.1 seconds to extend and 5.8 seconds to retract = 12.9 seconds total cycle time.

What size gas engine do I need to run a 11 GPM 2-stage hydraulic pump?

A two-stage hi-low log splitter pump unloads its high-volume stage at ~650 PSI and switches to its low-volume high-pressure stage up to 3,500 PSI. This requires at least 5.7 HP (212cc horizontal-shaft engine) and a 6.1+ gallon oil tank.
